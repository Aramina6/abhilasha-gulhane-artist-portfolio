Stepping into Wonderland: Exploring the Human Mind in My Debut San Francisco Exhibition

Art has always been my way of slowing down to analyze what is happening both around us and within our own minds—translating complex thoughts onto paper and canvas. I have long been fascinated by human behavior: why we make certain decisions, why we overthink, and how rapidly scaling technology quietly shapes our inner lives.

Recently, I moved to San Francisco near Potrero Avenue, and this new chapter inspired me to bring that internal “wonderland” to life. I am proud to share a major milestone in my studio practice: my first gallery exhibition. Two pieces from my new collection are currently on view in WONDERLAND, a group exhibition at The Drawing Room in the Mission District (599 Valencia Street), running through January 10, 2027.

Using geometric line work built from circles, triangles, and squares in earthy browns alongside sky and water blues, these works map the intersection of human thought and the natural world:

  • Mind Garden — Acrylic on canvas, 18 × 24 in, 2026. Inspired by the experience of hiking and being surrounded by nature, this painting explores how the mind connects with the world around us.
  • Between Worlds — Acrylic on canvas, 18 × 24 in, 2026. Thoughts can take us on journeys to places we've never been. A little sailboat drifts between waves and clouds, capturing the feeling of letting our imagination wander between different worlds.

Abhilasha Gulhane is a San Francisco-based painter inspired by nature, everyday experiences, and the human mind. Using simple geometric shapes, patterns, and earthy colors, she creates paintings that explore our connection with the world around us.

I warmly invite collectors, art advisors, and fellow art lovers to experience the work in person. The gallery is open during regular hours—Thursday through Sunday, 11am to 6pm—with no appointment required. You can also view the full WONDERLAND exhibition details on Eventbrite.

To follow along with my new San Francisco studio journey, connect with me on Instagram at @abhilasha.sfart or reach out directly at abhilashagulhane.com for private inquiries.
